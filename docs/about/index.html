About JauntOut

JauntOut helps you decide what to do near you in London. Tell it your postcode and who's going, and it suggests things one card at a time, starting with what's walkable or a short trip away.

Where the listings come from

Every day or two JauntOut reads the what's-on pages of 404 venues, organisers and councils across London (266 of them small local places like community centres, pubs, churches, libraries and societies), plus 87 Instagram accounts, a set of London newsletters, and events organisers send us. Right now that's about 1,620 things to do.

Descriptions are written in our own words, and Details takes you to the venue's or organiser's own page wherever we can find it, so you always book with them. Dates and prices can change, so check there before you go. Thank you to every one of these sources:

How JauntOut makes money

JauntOut is free to use, and listing an event is free. Some ticket links are affiliate links: if you buy a ticket after following one, we may earn a small commission from the ticket seller, at no extra cost to you. We only use them where that seller is the event's own ticket seller, and they never affect what we suggest or the order you see things in.

Your privacy

There are no accounts and no advertising cookies. Your postcode, saved ideas and choices stay on your phone. To improve JauntOut we count things anonymously: which screens are used and for how long, which ideas are saved or shared, what's searched for, and the postcode district people search from (for example "SE4", never your full postcode). We don't store IP addresses or anything that identifies you.

Weather comes from Open-Meteo, journey times from Transport for London, postcode lookups from postcodes.io and fonts from Google Fonts. Your phone asks them directly, so they see the request like any website would.

Venues and organisers

Running something people can turn up to? List your event, free. If we've got something wrong about your venue or event, or you'd rather we didn't list you, email hello@jauntout.com and we'll sort it quickly.
